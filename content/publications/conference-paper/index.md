Results 
Psychophysical analyses demonstrated that chromatic adaptation to yellow significantly shifted the color

(t(11)=−2.769, p=0.018), with a large effect size (Cohen's d=−0.799). This threshold increase (a rightward 
shift of −0.0801) was visualized in the psychometric curve, and the overall data distribu on in the box plot

Conclusion 
The threshold shift confirms the occurrence of fatigue in the Yellow-Blue Opponent Channel, consistent with 
the principles of Krauskopf et al. (1992). Notably, the data reveal substan al individual variability, ranging 
from severe fatigue to strong compensation (increased sensitivity), which suggests the operation of dynamic 
and distinct neural strategies for homeostasis. Persistent fatigue among spectacle-wearing subjects and 
extreme fatigue in subjects with uncorrected visual impairment indicate that sensory threshold stability is 
highly susceptible to cognitive load. These findings highlight the close link between basic sensory 
mechanisms and higher-order brain processes, suggesting that adaptation functions as a neuro-cognitive
